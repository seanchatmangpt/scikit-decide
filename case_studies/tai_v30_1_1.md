# TAI v30.1.1 — Challenger Forward-Deployment Case Study

## Standing and scope

This bounded case study turns user-supplied historical Technology Applications,
Inc. (TAI) brochure observations into an executable `scikit-decide`
deterministic-planning domain. It does **not** claim independent verification of
TAI's historical corporate statements, current corporate continuity, or live
customer outcomes.

Version: **30.1.1**

## Thesis

A Challenger Forward-Deployed Architect does not begin with an empty discovery
workshop. The architect compiles public business evidence and public ontology,
manufactures a provisional dependency-closed system, and arrives with the keys.
Private customer evidence then validates or falsifies the compiled model, binds
private parameters, grants authority, and activates the lawful transition.

## Planning pipeline

```text
public business evidence + public ontology
→ compile intended enterprise
→ admit public model
→ manufacture keys
→ validate or refuse local conformance
→ bind private parameters
→ admit runtime
→ grant authority
→ BRCE actuation intent
→ verify consequence
→ issue receipt
→ replay through BRCE intent
→ standing
```

The planner performs **SELECT** only. Generated artifacts belong to
**CONSTRUCT**. `brce_actuate` and `brce_replay` are modeled intents and do not
perform external side effects. Production execution must remain behind BRCE:
**zero unreceipted actuation**.

## Ontology bindings

The executable fixture binds:

- PROV-O `Entity` to admitted input evidence;
- PROV-O `Activity` to manufacture;
- PROV-O `Agent` and ODRL `Permission` to authority;
- DCTERMS `hasVersion` to case-study identity;
- OCEL `Event` to the receipt-bearing execution episode.

These bindings appear in deterministic receipts and are therefore part of the
verification surface rather than decorative metadata.

## TAI evidence represented

The admitted input fixture preserves brochure statements that TAI:

- began with a Navy engineering-services contract in 1977;
- worked across systems integration, telecommunications, software engineering,
  environmental services, and engineering services;
- delivered lifecycle work including design, installation, testing, training,
  operation, maintenance, and customer-site support;
- reported more than 500 employees serving customer installations.

## Acceptance

```bash
pytest -q tests/domains/test_tai_v30_1_1.py
python examples/tai_v30_1_1.py
```

The tests prove:

1. the positive path reaches standing only after BRCE intent, verified
   consequence, receipt, and replay;
2. BRCE actuation is refused before runtime admission and authority;
3. a falsified local model terminates without actuation and emits a typed refusal
   receipt;
4. receipt replay is byte-deterministic at the semantic payload boundary.

## Falsifiers

The case study's `ALIVE` standing is invalidated if the exact package revision
cannot import the domain, any declared transition bypasses authority or receipt
requirements, replay changes the receipt identity, or the negative-conformance
fixture reaches actuation.
