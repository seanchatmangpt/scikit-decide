# 30. The Post-LLM Career Canvas

The Post-LLM Career Canvas compresses the book into one operating surface.

Complete it in pencil, a document, or a graph tool. Revisit it monthly during active transition and quarterly during stable employment.

## 1. Market change

**What changed in the world that makes your capability more valuable now?**

Avoid general answers such as "AI is growing."

Name a concrete transition:

> Companies can generate AI output but cannot move it into bounded production workflows.

## 2. Target market

**Who experiences the problem urgently and has the ability to act?**

Include:

- company type;
- function;
- buyer;
- sponsor;
- affected users;
- blockers.

## 3. Outcome contract

**What change will you reliably produce?**

Template:

> Given **[admitted inputs]**, I produce **[outcome]** within **[boundary]**, validated by **[evidence]**, under **[authority]**.

## 4. Career Graph cluster

List your strongest connected nodes.

- outcomes;
- domains;
- systems;
- agents and tools;
- evidence;
- authority;
- market.

Write the edge sentence that joins them.

## 5. Five Layers profile

Estimate current and desired percentages.

| Layer | Current | Desired |
|---|---:|---:|
| Execution |  |  |
| Coordination |  |  |
| Judgment |  |  |
| Architecture |  |  |
| Standing |  |  |

Name the transition that would move value upward.

## 6. Personal agent economy

For your primary outcome, define:

- trigger;
- observation;
- admission;
- construction;
- validation;
- actuation;
- receipt;
- cache.

Assign each step to human, deterministic software, LLM, planner, or external service.

## 7. Irreducible configuration

Complete:

> I combine **[context]**, **[trust]**, **[system construction]**, **[evidence]**, and **[standing]** to produce **[outcome]** for **[market]**.

Then list the strongest plausible substitute and the edge it lacks.

## 8. Proof ladder

For each major claim, mark evidence level 0–6.

Select one claim to upgrade this month.

## 9. Public thesis

Write:

- one category lesson;
- one Challenger reframe;
- one role statement;
- one proof statement;
- one direct ask.

These sentences should form a coherent sequence.

## 10. Distribution system

List:

- audience size;
- five network segments;
- recurring content sources;
- publication cadence;
- referral packet;
- conversion metric.

Identify the attention source you can borrow and the original interpretation you can add.

## 11. Target-company pain map

For your top ten targets, record:

- observed signals;
- pain hypothesis;
- buyer;
- proof fit;
- discovery question;
- next action.

## 12. Evaluation contract

Define the fairest rigorous way to evaluate you.

Include:

- realistic problem;
- allowed tools;
- time;
- artifact;
- behavioral checks;
- failure conditions;
- replay evidence.

## 13. First ninety days

Write one receipt for each milestone:

- day 30: admitted map and baseline;
- day 60: working validated slice;
- day 90: one production outcome and roadmap.

Include falsifiers.

## 14. Constraints

Name:

- financial runway;
- time;
- health and energy;
- geography;
- work arrangement;
- compensation floor;
- authority requirements;
- ethical exclusions;
- intellectual-property boundaries.

Constraints are part of the problem, not evidence of insufficient ambition.

## 15. Standing

For the target capability, mark:

- UNKNOWN;
- PARTIAL_ALIVE;
- ALIVE;
- BLOCKED;
- BUILD_BROKEN;
- UNSUPPORTED;
- REFUSED.

State the exact evidence required for the next status transition.

## 16. Next reversible action

Choose one action that:

- can be completed within seven days;
- creates evidence;
- preserves options;
- tests a market hypothesis;
- does not require permission you do not have.

Write the completion receipt before beginning.

## Worked example: Agentic AI Architect

The following example shows how the sections correspond.

### Market change

LLMs and personal-agent systems make cognitive construction cheap, but companies still lack the operating layer that turns generated intent into reliable action.

### Target market

Large companies with visible AI investment, expensive cross-system workflows, executive sponsorship, and pressure to demonstrate production value.

### Outcome contract

> Given an admitted workflow and access to the relevant systems, I produce one bounded agent-enabled operating path within ninety days, validated through behavioral tests and production evidence, with consequential action restricted to authorized execution.

### Career Graph cluster

```text
Enterprise architecture
    + agent orchestration
    + formal planning
    + mechanical verification
    + public teaching
    + Fortune-scale experience
        → AI workflow transformation
```

### Five Layers profile

| Layer | Current | Desired |
|---|---:|---:|
| Execution | 35% | 20% |
| Coordination | 15% | 15% |
| Judgment | 20% | 20% |
| Architecture | 25% | 30% |
| Standing | 5% | 15% |

The desired shift is not away from technical reality. It is toward broader authority for outcomes.

### Personal agent economy

- trigger: target workflow or market signal;
- observation: documents, systems, interviews, and public evidence;
- admission: identity, freshness, scope, and constraints checked;
- construction: DSPy modules, coding agents, planning solvers;
- validation: tests, fixtures, simulations, and review;
- actuation: controlled broker or explicit human action;
- receipt: exact subject, command, result, and limitations;
- cache: domain models, policies, failures, and prior receipts.

### Irreducible configuration

> I combine long-range software architecture, high-throughput agent manufacturing, formal decision systems, evidence discipline, and an established professional audience to make emerging agent capabilities understandable and operational for large organizations.

### Public thesis

> OpenClaw shows that people want agents that act across tools. The next enterprise problem is deciding which actions are lawful, affordable, reliable, and reusable. I build that layer.

### Evaluation contract

A real workflow, normal agent tools, explicit constraints, a bounded build window, required negative cases, and a replayable result.

### First ninety days

- day 30: current-state workflow and cost map admitted;
- day 60: validated staging path with exception policy;
- day 90: controlled production outcome with receipt and reuse roadmap.

The example is specific enough to guide action while remaining falsifiable.

## Worked example: Domain professional, not software engineer

Consider a customer-success leader.

### Market change

AI can summarize accounts and draft communication, but teams still struggle to identify risk early and coordinate interventions consistently.

### Outcome contract

> Given current account, support, product-usage, and relationship observations, I produce a weekly prioritized risk review with evidence-backed intervention recommendations, approved by the account owner, and measured against retention outcomes.

### Career Graph cluster

```text
Customer relationships
    + account-risk judgment
    + workflow design
    + AI-assisted synthesis
    + executive communication
        → scalable retention operations
```

### Personal agent economy

The model gathers and summarizes signals. A deterministic rules layer checks known thresholds. The customer-success leader judges relationship context and approves action. The system records which intervention was selected and what happened.

### Target role

AI-Enabled Customer Success Operations Lead or Customer Intelligence Architect.

This example demonstrates that the canvas is not limited to engineering careers.

## Canvas review questions

After completing the canvas, challenge it.

### Correspondence

Does every role claim map to evidence?

### Consequence

Does the outcome matter enough for a buyer to act?

### Authority

Can the role actually produce the outcome in the proposed organization?

### Differentiation

Is the configuration difficult to substitute, or merely described with new language?

### Discoverability

Can recruiters and search systems find the role?

### Sustainability

Can you operate the system without destructive workload?

### Learning

Does every campaign and project update the graph?

## Turn the canvas into projections

One completed canvas should generate:

- your LinkedIn headline;
- your About section;
- your résumé summary;
- a role brief;
- an executive introduction;
- a recruiter explanation;
- a proof backlog;
- a content series;
- a first-ninety-days plan;
- interview questions for the employer.

Do not write each projection independently. Derive them from the canvas so the identity remains coherent.

## The monthly release

Version the canvas.

```text
career-canvas-v1.0.md
career-canvas-v1.1.md
career-canvas-v2.0.md
```

A minor version reflects new evidence or language. A major version reflects a meaningful market or identity change.

Keep a change log:

- new observation;
- changed hypothesis;
- new evidence;
- role shift;
- removed claim;
- new constraint;
- next experiment.

This turns career evolution into an inspectable learning path.

## The canvas test

A complete canvas should let another person answer:

1. What changed?
2. What does this person do?
3. Why are they difficult to substitute?
4. What proof exists?
5. Who should hire them?
6. How should they be evaluated?
7. What will happen in the first ninety days?

Where the answers remain vague, the career system remains under-specified.

Do not decorate the canvas.

Use it to decide what to build next.
