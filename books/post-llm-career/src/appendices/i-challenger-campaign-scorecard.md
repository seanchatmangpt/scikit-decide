| Dimension | 0 | 4 | Score |

## Conversion ratios

Calculate:

```text
Profile inspection / relevant reach
Evidence inspection / profile inspection
Qualified conversation / evidence inspection
Evaluation / qualified conversation
Offer / evaluation
```

The ratios identify the failed transition.

- Low profile inspection: the post or identity is not compelling.
- Low evidence inspection: the profile does not route to proof.
- Low conversation rate: proof may lack relevance or invitation.
- Low evaluation rate: role, budget, or sponsorship may be absent.
- Low offer rate: evaluation, fit, compensation, or standing needs review.

## Weekly falsifier review

Complete these sentences:

- The campaign assumes that ________________.
- Evidence supporting the assumption this week: ________________.
- Evidence against it: ________________.
- The strongest alternative explanation is ________________.
- The smallest test next week is ________________.

## Weekly decision

Choose one item in each category.

### Preserve

What is working and should remain unchanged?

### Repair

Which failed transition has the highest leverage?

### Stop

Which activity creates output without market movement?

### Create

Which missing asset or relationship would unlock the next transition?

## Campaign standing

Choose one:

- `UNKNOWN` — insufficient observation;
- `PARTIAL_ALIVE` — some qualified transitions observed;
- `ALIVE` — repeated correspondence from teaching to opportunity;
- `BLOCKED` — external dependency prevents progress;
- `BUILD_BROKEN` — campaign assets or routing fail;
- `UNSUPPORTED` — thesis not supported by current evidence;
- `REFUSED` — opportunity violates explicit constraints.

State the exact subject and time window. A viral post is not an `ALIVE` hiring campaign unless it creates the intended transitions.
