## Scoring guidance

### Representation

1. Mostly tacit, relational, or inaccessible.
5. Explicit state, actions, constraints, and outcomes.

### Data

1. Missing, stale, or untrusted.
5. Accessible, current, and attributable.

### Repeatability

1. Unique situation.
5. Common recurring pattern.

### Evaluation

1. No clear acceptance standard.
5. Executable or objective checks.

### Reversibility

1. Difficult or impossible to reverse.
5. Draft or sandbox action.

### Consequence

1. Minimal harm if wrong.
5. Material financial, legal, safety, or reputational impact.

### Authority complexity

1. Individual can act directly.
5. Multiple approvals or regulated standing required.

### Reuse

1. Little future value.
5. Policy or system can serve many future cases.

## Classification

- **Class A — Deterministic conversion:** explicit rules and strong evaluation.
- **Class B — Agent-assisted production:** flexible construction with validation.
- **Class C — Policy manufacturing:** repeated decisions under constraints.
- **Class D — Human-standing work:** relationship, authority, values, or consequence dominate.
