# Appendix G. Post-LLM Résumé and Role-Brief Templates

These templates are projections of the Career Canvas. Complete the canvas first. Replace every bracketed field with evidence-bounded language.

## Résumé headline

```text
[Target role] | [Business consequence] | [Searchable technical/domain terms]
```

Example:

> Agentic AI Architect | Turning model output into bounded enterprise workflows | MCP, A2A, planning, evaluation, governance

## Professional summary

```text
[Role identity] with [years/range of relevant experience] across [domains or environments].
I design/build/operate [system category] that produces [business outcome].
My operating method combines [three to five differentiating capabilities].
Recent evidence includes [two or three proof objects with standing].
Targeting [level and role families] in [preferred environment].
```

## Outcome-system bullet

```text
[Action and ownership] a [system] that transformed [admitted input/current state]
into [accepted outcome], using [relevant architecture], validated by [evidence],
resulting in [measured consequence].
```

Example:

> Designed an agent-assisted migration factory that converted source schemas into tested transformation plans, preserved human approval for production changes, and reused validated mappings across repeated cases; evidence included fixture suites, exact-head build receipts, and replayable migration reports.

## Proof-aware metric

```text
Improved [metric] from [baseline] to [result] for [subject/workload] over [period],
including [important cost or review boundary].
```

Avoid metrics without a baseline, subject, or denominator.

## Agent-leverage disclosure

Use language that establishes your contribution.

```text
Defined the objective, architecture, constraints, and evaluation for a multi-agent
construction workflow; agents produced candidate artifacts, while I admitted context,
reviewed evidence, authorized changes, and owned the release result.
```

## Alternative-evaluation request

> I understand the purpose of the standard screen: the company needs a consistent technical signal. The target role also depends on [missing capabilities]. I propose a bounded work sample or portfolio execution review using normal tools, explicit constraints, and replayable evidence. I am asking for a more role-valid test, not a less rigorous one.

## Final résumé audit

- Can a recruiter identify the target role in ten seconds?
- Can an executive identify the business consequence?
- Can a technical leader find evidence?
- Is your own contribution explicit?
- Are agent and tool claims bounded?
- Are metrics contextualized?
- Are adjacent role projections consistent?
- Is the desired next action stated?
