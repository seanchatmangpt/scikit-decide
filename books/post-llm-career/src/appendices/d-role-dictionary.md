# Appendix D. Post-LLM Role Dictionary

This dictionary provides searchable labels and functional definitions. Titles vary by company. Evaluate the owned system rather than the name alone.

## Agentic AI Architect

Designs the end-to-end system connecting objectives, models, tools, memory, planning, evaluation, authority, economics, and evidence.

**Primary evidence:** architecture plus a bounded production workflow.

## Agent Systems Engineer

Builds and operates agent runtimes, tool integrations, state, orchestration, and event flows.

**Primary evidence:** working services, traces, recovery, and production behavior.

## Agent Experience Architect

Designs user experiences for delegation, clarification, progress, approval, intervention, and trust.

**Primary evidence:** usable product surfaces and behavioral research.

## Agent Reliability Engineer

Owns evaluation, test corpora, monitoring, failure classification, refusal, fallback, replay, and incident repair.

**Primary evidence:** evaluation harnesses and repaired failure traces.

## Agent Authority Engineer

Designs capability scopes, identity, policy, approval, brokered actuation, and receipts.

**Primary evidence:** enforced authority paths and audit-ready execution.

## Decision Systems Architect

Models states, actions, constraints, probabilities, resources, goals, plans, and policies.

**Primary evidence:** formal models, solver integration, simulation, and policy performance.

## Semantic Systems Engineer

Builds structured semantic programs, ontologies, schemas, retrieval, classification, examples, and validation.

**Primary evidence:** improved structured accuracy and interoperable knowledge assets.

## AI Governance Engineer

Translates legal, security, ethical, and organizational policy into operational controls and evidence.

**Primary evidence:** executable policy, approval, retention, and audit mechanisms.

## AI Transformation Architect

Connects business pain, operating-model redesign, architecture, portfolio selection, economics, adoption, and executive decision-making.

**Primary evidence:** cross-functional transformation with measured outcomes.

## Agent Product Manager

Owns agent product outcomes, user needs, capability boundaries, evaluation, and roadmap.

**Primary evidence:** product decisions connected to behavior and customer outcomes.

## Agent Evaluation Engineer

Designs datasets, scenarios, metrics, judges, adversarial tests, and release gates for model and agent behavior.

**Primary evidence:** evaluation validity and production correlation.

## AI Cost and Performance Engineer

Optimizes model routing, context, caching, latency, quality, and total workflow economics.

**Primary evidence:** measured cost-quality improvement under stable acceptance criteria.

## AI Integration Architect

Connects models and agents to enterprise APIs, data, identity, events, and legacy systems.

**Primary evidence:** reliable cross-system integration with bounded access.

## AI Solutions Architect

Works with customers to discover needs, design solutions, build demonstrations, define deployment, and align stakeholders.

**Primary evidence:** customer-facing architecture and successful implementation path.

## Talent Intelligence Architect

Designs evidence-rich systems for role definition, candidate discovery, matching, evaluation, and hiring-flow improvement.

**Primary evidence:** improved quality, speed, fairness, and candidate experience.

## Work-System Architect

Models organizational flow, constraints, handoffs, state, automation, and outcome ownership.

**Primary evidence:** reduced cycle time and coordination burden.

## Knowledge System Engineer

Creates maintainable knowledge structures usable by both humans and machines, including provenance and lifecycle.

**Primary evidence:** retrieval quality, reuse, and reduced knowledge decay.

## Personal Agent Operator

Operates a bounded personal or professional agent economy around recurring outcomes.

**Primary evidence:** improved outcomes, lower total attention, and explicit authority boundaries.
