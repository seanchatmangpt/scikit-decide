# About the Author

Sean Chatman is a software and solution architect focused on agentic systems, AI-native products, formal planning, mechanical verification, and the operating structures required to turn generated intelligence into accountable outcomes.

His professional software career spans more than twenty-five years, including work associated with Intuit, Disney, Riot Games, and MySpace. His current body of work explores agent orchestration, MCP and A2A interoperability, probabilistic planning, ontology-driven generation, WebAssembly process evidence, language-server verification, reusable intelligence, and receipted execution.

Sean also writes and teaches publicly about the post-LLM economy: how agents change software, work, hiring, organizational design, and professional value. His LinkedIn audience includes more than 21,000 followers.

The central idea connecting the work is the Chatman Equation:

\[
A = \mu(O^*)
\]

An artifact or action should be manufactured from admitted observation through a lawful process. Consequential action should produce a receipt that establishes identity, authority, result, and replay.

This book applies that architecture to careers. It asks how a person can become legible, valuable, and accountable when intelligence is abundant and the inherited labor-market map no longer describes the available work.
