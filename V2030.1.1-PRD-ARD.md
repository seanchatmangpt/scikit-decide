**Ecosystem role:** EXPLORE / planner league / benchmark laboratory

**Release:** Chatman Ecosystem v2030.1.1
**Standing conferred:** `UNKNOWN`.

## Product requirement

`autofde-lab` SHALL be the exploration system that searches, benchmarks, falsifies, and ranks candidate Forward Deployed Engineering policies before they can graduate into the exploit-only product. Its v2030.1.1 purpose is to maximize what the ecosystem can learn without granting laboratory planners ambient production authority.

## Required capabilities

1. Maintain a planner league spanning deterministic search, symbolic planning, optimization/constraint solving, heuristics, LLM candidate producers, RL policies, and ensembles where useful.
2. Preserve the distinction `Planner != Policy != Role != Agent`.
3. Represent policy as:

   `Planner × Parameters × Objective × ObservationProjection × ActionProjection`.
4. Represent episode as world + roles + policies + information partitions + authority model.
5. Benchmark policies across `gymact` worlds for success, cost, latency, reversibility, violations, evidence completeness, and portability.
6. Support adversarial, chaos, counterexample, and mutation scenarios rather than happy-path-only scoring.
7. Compare alternative cloud, marketplace, network, security, process, and enterprise architectures through DfCM.
8. Produce graduation packets containing exact observations, policy identity, benchmark corpus, results, falsifiers, limits, and required downstream admission.
9. Keep research/lab result standing distinct from production AutoFDE standing.

## Architecture requirements

```text
benchmark corpus + gym world + objective/constraints
        -> observation/action projections
        -> planner league
        -> candidate policies/architectures
        -> repeated episodes + adversarial episodes
        -> metric/constraint evaluation
        -> Pareto / admissible candidate frontier
        -> graduation packet
        -> downstream formal/runtime admission
```

LLMs are optional compilers/oracles/candidate producers. They SHALL NOT be treated as proof or production authority.

## DfCM requirement

Exploration SHALL optimize the maximal reversible lawful possibility graph before downstream exploit selection. Failed candidates are retained as topology/evidence so the same dead edges are not repeatedly rediscovered without a new hypothesis.

## Acceptance for v2030.1.1

- materially different planners can run against the same admitted gym subject;
- results are reproducible under pinned world/policy/seed/toolchain identities where deterministic replay is claimed;
- benchmark reports include positive and adversarial evidence;
- hard constraint violations eliminate candidates rather than merely lowering a score;
- candidate rankings expose objective tradeoffs instead of hiding them in one opaque aggregate;
- at least representative multi-cloud and marketplace architecture problems can be searched end-to-end in gyms;
- graduation into `autofde` requires explicit evidence and does not transfer lab authority.

## Falsifiers

The architecture is falsified if one planner's output is treated as the answer without comparison where alternatives exist; if benchmark success grants production actuation; if simulator artifacts are presented as live-cloud evidence; or if evaluation cannot reproduce the subject/policy/world identities that produced a score.

## Ecosystem contract

`autofde-lab` explores. `gymact` supplies executable worlds/actions. `bcinr` may supply deterministic mechanics. `autofde` is the exploit-only product and must independently admit any graduated policy or architecture before consequential use.
